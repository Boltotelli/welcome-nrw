# Kingshot notice icons

22 game-item preview PNGs observed in the StratForge notice-builder UI and exported through the browser's page-assets inventory on 2026-11-04. Original source paths are `/kingshot/items/`, `/kingshot/resources/` and `/kingshot/speedups/` at https://stratforge.tools . Filenames were preserved. Only Kingshot item images were selected; no StratForge brand assets, avatars or unrelated files were copied.

Game artwork belongs to its respective rights holders (Kingshot / Century Games). These previews identify the game items in a fan-tool editor. The copied text contains game tags or Unicode characters, not these images. Source filenames are not assumed to equal the inserted item IDs: Gem uses `item_icon_100001.png` as its preview, but inserts `<item_icon_101>`.

All 27 item tags and 32 private-use characters were checked by clicking their reference buttons and reading the resulting local draft. The reference draft was restored to empty; no templates or announcements were published. Six item-tag entries and fourteen special-character entries have no preview image in the reference; they retain their full names. Private-use characters may render as boxes outside Kingshot. Actual in-game support remains labelled untested.
